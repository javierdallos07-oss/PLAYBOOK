## Filming Checklist

Read this before every recording session.

### Before you hit record
- Breathe: 3 slow breaths, in through the nose, out through the mouth
- Shoulders back and down, chest open, chin level
- Smile before you start talking. Hold it for one second, then go.
- Phone at eye level, camera lens at your eyes, not below your chin
- Light in front of you (window or ring light), never behind you
- Clean background, lens wiped

### Camera angles
- **Talking head:** phone on a tripod at eye level, framed from mid chest up. Use for tips and money videos.
- **Walk and talk:** hold the phone at arm's length, slightly above eye level, walk slowly. Use for neighborhood videos.
- **Over the shoulder:** someone films from behind you as you point at a detail. Use for home inspection moments.
- **B-roll:** 3 second clips of doors, kitchens, streets, signs. Film 10 of these every session.

### Voice and delivery
- **Hook:** say it with energy, a little louder and faster
- **Body:** slow down, warm and calm, like explaining to a friend
- **Numbers:** pause right before a number so it lands
- **CTA:** smile, friendly and direct
- If you stumble, pause, breathe, start the sentence again. Cut it in editing.

## Gym

Tuesday is Session A, Thursday is Session B. Warm up 5 minutes first: arm circles, 10 kettlebell deadlifts, 10 bodyweight squats. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Pick a weight where the last 2 reps are hard but clean.

### Progression
| Week | Sets x reps | Plank hold |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1, Nov 16 | 3 x 10 | 3 x 30 sec |
| Week 2, Nov 23 | 3 x 12 | 3 x 40 sec |
| Week 3, Nov 30 | 4 x 10, go heavier | 3 x 45 sec |
| Week 4, Dec 7 | 4 x 12 | 3 x 60 sec |

Pull-ups: as many clean reps as you can each set.

### Session A (Tuesday) · 6 exercises
- **Seated shoulder press** (20 to 25 lb each): sit tall, press until arms are straight, lower to ear level.
- **Lateral raise** (10 to 15 lb): soft elbows, lift to shoulder height, lower slowly for 3 seconds.
- **Hammer curl** (20 to 25 lb): palms face each other, elbows pinned to your sides.
- **Overhead tricep press** (the 35 lb): both hands on one dumbbell, lower behind your head, elbows point up.
- **Decline sit-up:** hook your feet under the pads, lower slowly, curl up without pulling your neck. When it gets easy, hold the 20 lb on your chest.
- **Superman:** lie face down, lift arms, chest and legs together, hold 3 seconds at the top.

### Session B (Thursday) · 6 exercises
- **Pull-up:** start from a full hang, pull your chin over the bar. Can't do 5 yet? Jump up and lower slowly.
- **Dumbbell bench press** (35 lb, or the adjustables at 40 to 45): lower to chest level, press up, wrists straight.
- **Bent-over row** (the 35 lb, one arm at a time): flat back, pull the dumbbell to your hip.
- **Kettlebell swing:** hinge at the hips, snap them forward, arms just guide the bell to chest height. This one works your lower back.
- **Forearm plank:** straight line from head to heels, squeeze abs and glutes.
- **Hanging knee raise:** hang still, bring knees up to hip level, no swinging.
